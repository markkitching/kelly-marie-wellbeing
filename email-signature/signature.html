Adding it to Gmail

  1. Open Gmail, click the cog, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab scroll to Signature and click Create new.
  3. Paste the signature into the box.
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

Adding it to Outlook

  1. Go to Settings, then Mail, then Compose and reply (or Signatures).
  2. Create a new signature, paste it in, and save.
